# Aster-9 Internal Hypoxia Reporter Assay

> Controlled demonstration document. Aster-9, Lumen-R7, and Vela-B are
> fictional names created for K-LIB evaluation. This is not a real laboratory
> or clinical protocol and must not be used for biological work.

## Culture eligibility

Use an Aster-9 reporter culture only between passages 6 and 11 inclusive and
only when confluence is between 72% and 78%. Record the passage and confluence
before adding the reporter reagent.

## Reporter priming

Add 7.5 microliters of Lumen-R7 to each 2.0 milliliters of culture medium.
Incubate for 14 minutes at 33 degrees Celsius before beginning the hypoxia
pulse.

## Hypoxia pulse

Expose the primed culture to 1.7% oxygen for 42 minutes at 33 degrees Celsius
while maintaining 5% carbon dioxide. The 42-minute timer starts only after the
chamber reaches 1.7% oxygen.

## Vela-B wash

Vela-B contains 18 mM HEPES, 135 mM sodium chloride, and 4.5 mM potassium
chloride, adjusted to pH 7.28. Perform exactly two washes using 3.0 milliliters
per wash and a contact time of 20 seconds per wash.

## Reporter readout

Excite the reporter at 405 nm and measure emission at 472 nm. A valid positive
response is a normalized 472-nm ratio of at least 1.35 at minute 11 after the
second Vela-B wash.

Abort the run if either condition occurs:

1. Chamber temperature exceeds 34.2 degrees Celsius.
2. The normalized 472-nm ratio exceeds 2.10 before minute 4.

An aborted run must not be repeated using the same culture.

## Recovery sequence

After a valid readout, recover the culture for 6 minutes in ambient oxygen,
then for 9 minutes at 5% carbon dioxide and 33 degrees Celsius. The order must
not be reversed.
