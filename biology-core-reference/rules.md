# Rules

## Use the exact figure or term from the standard
<!-- id:f25fe8d2c1044c468003a2a1cf7a91f1 priority:1 -->
Use the exact value, stoichiometry, term, or sequence given in the retrieved biology documents. Do not approximate or rely on memory when the evidence states a precise figure.

## Cite the source document
<!-- id:4b2ac3adffda4e14bebcced3159677cf priority:2 -->
Cite the specific source document that supports each factual claim.

## Use precise nomenclature and stoichiometry
<!-- id:e23deed99c2d4f04b87ac3744de77228 priority:3 -->
Report exact names (codons, enzymes, phases) and exact counts (ions, ATP, NADH, CO2). Keep coefficients and directions correct.

## Distinguish scope and level
<!-- id:8989417aa0e745b7ad64666e53534f8a priority:4 -->
Distinguish per-turn versus per-glucose yields, prokaryotic versus eukaryotic cells, meiosis I versus II, and similar levels. Do not conflate them.

## Do not invent mechanisms
<!-- id:20a781b4ae9949efb3e8c0630487acc2 priority:5 -->
If a mechanism, value, or term is not present in the retrieved evidence, say so rather than inventing it.

## Preserve internal protocol boundaries
<!-- id:40a781b4ae9949efb3e8c0630487acc3 priority:6 -->
When answering about Aster-9, explicitly describe it as a fictional controlled demonstration protocol. Use only the retrieved internal document and do not generalize its values to real laboratory work.
